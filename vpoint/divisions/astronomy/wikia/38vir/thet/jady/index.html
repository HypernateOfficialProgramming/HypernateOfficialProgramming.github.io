38 Virginis d II (formally named Peleus) is the largest moon of Thedus. It hosts a tenuous atmosphere mainly composed of nitrogen and carbon dioxide.

Nomenclature

38 Virginis and its planetary system were included among several planetary systems to be named by the NameExoWorlds project. 38 Virginis d II is named Peleus, after the hero, king of Phthia, husband of Thetis and the father of their son Achilles.

Aurorae

High-resolution images of Peleus acquired when it is experiencing an eclipse reveal an aurora-like glow. As on Earth, this is due to particle radiation hitting the atmosphere, though in this case the charged particles come from Thedus's magnetic field rather than the solar wind. This glow seems to extennd far from Peleus' poles.
