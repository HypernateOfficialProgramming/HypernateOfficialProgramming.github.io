38 Virginis d (formally named Thedus) is an exoplanet orbiting the star 38 Virginis, located in the constellation Virgo. It is a large gas giant primarily composed of hydrogen (H2).

38 Virginis d orbits its parent star at a distance of about 1.30 AU with an orbital period of approximately 456.91 Earth days.

The planet's rotation makes it an oblate spheroid, which is flattened at the poles and bulging at the equator. Its equatorial radius is more than 7.7% longer than the polar radius.

Nomenclature

38 Virginis and its planetary system were included among several planetary systems to be named by the NameExoWorlds project. 38 Virginis d is named Thedus, after Thetis, a figure from Greek mythology who mainly appears as a sea nymph, a goddess of water, and one of the 50 Nereids.

The minor planet 17 Thetis is also named after Thetis, and differs only by spelling.

Coincidentally, the planet has a similar name to Thaedus, a character in Invincible.

Moons

Thedus has 17 known moons, 3 of which have formal names. Peleus, the largest of the three, hosts a major atmosphere composed of nitrogen, carbon dioxide, and oxygen.
