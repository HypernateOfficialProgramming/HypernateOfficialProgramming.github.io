38 Virginis b (formally named Galitaea) is a super-Jupiter exoplanet orbiting within the habitable zone of the star 38 Virginis about 108.5 light-years (33.26 parsecs) from Earth in the constellation Virgo.

38 Virginis b has a circular orbit around its host star, having a period of 738.916 days at a semi-major axis of 1.79 AU. This separation is within the habitable zone of its host star. As of 2025, it was the only known planet around a F-type star that orbits in the habitable zone at all points of time.

Nomenclature

38 Virginis and its planetary system were included among several planetary systems to be named by the NameExoWorlds project. 38 Virginis b is named Galitaea, after Galatea, a Nereid who loved the shepherd Acis, and was loved by the cyclops Polyphemus.

The moon of Neptune Galatea is also named after Galatea, and differs only by spelling.

Moons

Galitaea has 73 known moons, 9 of which have formal names. Neopandora, one of the moons, hosts a very diverse biosphere and bioluminescent flora.

Neopandora itself appears to glow a faint greenish from orbit.
