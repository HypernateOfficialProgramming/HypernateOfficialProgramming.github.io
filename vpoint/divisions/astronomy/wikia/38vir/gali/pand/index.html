38 Virginis b II (formally named Neopandora) is the third largest moon of Galitaea. It hosts an atmosphere mainly composed of water vapor.

Nomenclature

38 Virginis and its planetary system were included among several planetary systems to be named by the NameExoWorlds project. 38 Virginis b II is named Neopandora, a nod to the fictional moon Pandora.

Surface features

The surface of Neopandora is covered in massive oceans, with entire continents that remain mostly unexplored.

Diverse, alien ecosystems populate these continents, from lush jungles to open plains. Various forms of plant life on Neopandora exhibit very bright forms of bioluminescence visible from orbit.

Due to Neopandora's lack of oxygen, it is impossible for earth-based life to exist on Neopandora without a sealed enclosure.

Establishments

Blueville Station

Blueville Station is a Vectorpoint scientific research station located on Continent B. It is the southernmost point established on Neopandora.

Individual buildings contained the dorms, galley, recreational center, post office and labs for monitoring the upper and lower atmosphere, along with wildlife.

New Wycliffe

New Wycliffe is a tourist attraction aimed at giving tourists a view of the extraterrestrial lifeforms on Neopandora. It covers around 990 acres of land and resides on subcontinent D.
