38 Virginis c (formally named Eruantur) is an exoplanet orbiting the star 38 Virginis, located in the constellation Virgo. It is an ice giant primarily composed of hydrogen (H2) and methane (CH4).

38 Virginis c orbits its parent star at a distance of about 2.27 AU with an orbital period of approximately 1058.33 Earth days.

Nomenclature

38 Virginis and its planetary system were included among several planetary systems to be named by the NameExoWorlds project. 38 Virginis c is named Eruantur, a latin verb form meaning "they may be rooted out", while its moon is named Sacrare, meaning "to consecrate".

Moons

Eruantur hosts one moon, Sacrare. The tidal forces experienced by Sacrare creates significant tidal heating within its interior, melting a significant amount of its mantle and core.
