38 Virginis e (formally named Galena) is an exoplanet orbiting the star 38 Virginis, located in the constellation Virgo. It is a desert-like rocky planet with a definite atmosphere that is primarily oxygen (O2) and nitrogen (N2). At the average surface level the atmospheric pressure is 3.41 times higher than that of Earth's.

38 Virginis e orbits its parent star at a distance of about 0.79 AU with an orbital period of approximately 217.54 Earth days.

The planet has an obliquity of 55.7°, leading to extreme variations in sunlight in certain areas.

Nomenclature

38 Virginis and its planetary system were included among several planetary systems to be named by the NameExoWorlds project. 38 Virginis e is named Galena, after Galene, a minor goddess in Greek mythology personifying calm seas.

The minor planet 427 Galene is also named after the mythological Galene, and differs only by spelling.

Moons

Galena currently has three satellites. These three are Margarites, Thallassion, and Amphrite.

All of them were discovered via Mintyl-2's PANOPTES, a wide-range camera used during various missions to 38 Virginis.
