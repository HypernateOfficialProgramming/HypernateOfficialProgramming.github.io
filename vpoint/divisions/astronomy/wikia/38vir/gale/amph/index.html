38 Virginis e I (formally named Amphrite) is the largest moon of Galena. It hosts a thick atmosphere dominated by nitrogen. It is the only known object in the 38 Virginis system besides Neopandora with stable bodies of surface water.

Nomenclature

38 Virginis and its planetary system were included among several planetary systems to be named by the NameExoWorlds project. 38 Virginis e I is named Amphrite, after Amphitrite, goddess of the sea, queen of the sea, and consort of Poseidon.

The minor planet 29 Amphitrite is also named after Amphitrite, and differs only by spelling.

Surface features

Following Mintyl-4's flybys of Amphrite, it was discovered that the moon had oceans of liquid water blanketing the entirety of its surface.
