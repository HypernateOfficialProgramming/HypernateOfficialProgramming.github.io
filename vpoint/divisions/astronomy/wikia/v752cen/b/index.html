V752 Centauri (AB) b, also referred to as V752 Centauri b, or Centaurus b, is an exoplanet orbiting the W Ursae Majoris type contact stars V752 A & B in the constellation Centaurus.

Physical properties

Orbital parameters

V752 Centauri (AB) b orbits V752 Centauri (AB) every 19.64 Earth days at a distance of about 0.17 AU, nearly 6 times closer to V752 Centauri (AB) than Earth is to the Sun. It is unlikely to have stable orbits for moons.

Mass, radius, and composition

V752 Centauri (AB) b is likely a solid planet made of carbon-rich material rather than the oxygen-rich material that makes up the terrestrial planets in the Solar System. In this case, roughly three fifths of the planet's mass would be carbon, much of which may be in the form of diamond as a result of the temperatures and pressures in the planet's interior.

Surface conditions

The equilibrium temperature of V752 Centauri (AB) b is estimated to be about 869 K (596 °C; 1,105 °F). This does not take into account tidal locking, making the day side likely 1800 K (1526.85°C; 2780.33°F) instead.

Atmosphere

A dicarbon outflow was detected in the atmosphere of V752 Centauri (AB) b, indicating carbon is being ablated from its surface. This outflow may lead to a green tail protruding from the planet.
