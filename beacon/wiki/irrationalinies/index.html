Description

The Irrationalinies are an unidentified form of matter that spontaneously form periodic or otherwising repeating properties depending on their mass.

A theory to their existence proposes they are an unstable form of quantum superposition that is kept stable by an unknown fifth force.

There are currently 9 known groups of this class of substance:

  • Ordinaries
  • Sinindes
  • Cosenides
  • Tanonides
  • Secanides
  • Febrides
  • SuperFebrides
  • Logarides
  • Ultratanonides

Almost every element of the Irrationalinies appear to have a non-integer effective baryon number arising from stabilized superposition.

Notable Examples

Skirtium

Skirtium is the most observed form of Irrationalinie, having an effective Z of \(|\psi\rangle = \sqrt{2 - \sqrt{2}}\,|1\rangle + \sqrt{\sqrt{2} - 1}\,|2\rangle\). It most commonly collapses into Hydrogen or Helium if observed in a normal environment.

Skirtium forms aromatic bonds with almost any element without a filled electron shell, even if non-cyclical, due to the non-integer electron count.

Thirvossenne

Thirvossenne is the second most observed form of Irrationalinie, having an effective Z of \(|\psi\rangle = \sqrt{0.97}\,|-826\rangle + \sqrt{0.03}\,|-825\rangle\). It most commonly collapses into extremely numerous amounts of Hydrogen, Helium, and other elements if observed in a normal environment.

It is extremely massive for being the second most observed Irrationalinie and it is unknown how it keeps itself stable.

Thirvossenne is antimatter-dominated.
