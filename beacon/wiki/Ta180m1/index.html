Tantalum-180m1

Tantalum-180m1 is an isotope of Tantalum. He resembles baseline Tantalum in appearance, though he is much darker, has a triangular robotic eye, and a head tuft capacitor rather than a capacitor earring.

Personality

Tantalum-180m1 is the secondary engineer of the Beacon Coalition, specializing in robotics and computation.

He fixed up Titanium-46 after she had fallen from the sky due to an unknown cause.
