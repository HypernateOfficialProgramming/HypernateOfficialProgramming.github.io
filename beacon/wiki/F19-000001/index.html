Fluorine - 000001

Fluorine is the lightest halogen of the so-called "Prime Universe." She is a dim yellow based on the colour of fluorine in liquid phase. She is a dentist and sometimes wears a head mirror.

Personality

Unlike most cases of Fluorine around existence, Fluorine-000001 had become a genocidal dictator ruling over the "One True Empire," an unidentified faction that may or may not be dangerous to all of existence.

Anomalous Abilities

  • She is able to generate pure energy out of her hand in the form of a devestating beam, destroying all matter that comes in contact with it.
  • She rules over an empire encompassing an entire celestial body where everything treats her similarly to a supreme deity.

Notes

  • Fluorine-000001 is incredibly racist against basically all metals.
  • She is temporarily immortal, although for some convoluted reasons that did involve Berkelium dying.
  • She pretends to be a deity and everyone acts as if she is one. This is more of propaganda than reality, but she may have anomalous properties tied to her.
  • She is somewhat depressed and sleep-deprived.
  • She is known as a "fuckin’ asshole," even in her personal life.
  • She seems to have a negative relationship with @yeetmoretbr, a similar deity-like being, and @ecmorsote.
